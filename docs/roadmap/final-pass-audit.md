# Final Product Completion & Experience Pass: audit and proposed sequence

> Status: **audit for approval**, 2026-09-29, at `d64a764` (M0–M8 complete).
> Nothing here is built yet. The sequence in §15 is a proposal; §16 lists
> the decisions it needs from the owner.

## 0. Method

Six read-only reviews ran in parallel, one per group of areas. Five read the
code, docs and tests. The sixth ran all four built apps against the seeded
dev database and took 196 screenshots at 1440×900 and 390×844, with
axe-core, overflow, alt-text, heading, target-size and keyboard checks on 75
routes (150 page states). Overlapping findings are merged below; the
original review ids are in brackets so evidence can be traced. The load-
bearing claims were re-checked by hand: PB-01 by reading the save and
publish code; CO-04 and ORD-01 by searching the order reads and the
notification enum; DB-05 in `setStorefrontLive`; MK-02 in `plan-features.ts`;
and RA-01, VIS-06 and VIS-09 in the screenshots.

**Classes** (exactly one per item): **Core**: missing core functionality ·
**UX**: UX deficiency · **Visual**: visual deficiency · **Adv**: advanced
feature. **Blocker** is a separate flag: _yes_ means launching real
merchants without it is broken, unsafe, misleading, or legally or
commercially unacceptable. **Size:** S ≈ ≤1 day, M ≈ 2–4 days, L ≈ a week or
more, each including tests.

**Constraints that apply to every item** and are not repeated per row:

- Keep the Site Engine / Commerce Engine separation (import-graph test).
- Keep tenancy: RLS, per-role grants and RBAC checked server-side.
- Money stays in integer minor units.
- Orders stay immutable snapshots.
- Keep the pricing-hash re-check at `beginPayment`.
- Keep stock and discount reservation under locks.
- Webhooks stay idempotent.
- Cookies stay host-only, and the nonce CSP stays in place.
- The storefront ships no client JavaScript (budget tests).
- Cache invalidation goes through the outbox and invalidation log (ADR-0034).
- Keep step-up and audit on sensitive writes.
- Entitlements never branch on plan names.

## 1. What is already solid (don't redo)

- **Accessibility and responsiveness:**
  - 0 axe violations on all 150 page states;
  - no horizontal overflow at 390 px;
  - skip link first, one `h1`/`main`, alt text everywhere, visible focus;
  - contrast derivation with regression tests;
  - mobile dashboard with a bottom bar and card tables.
- **Commerce correctness:**
  - pure pricing engine with a quote hash and typed change conflicts;
  - reservations under ordered locks;
  - immutable order snapshots;
  - bounded refunds with restock;
  - partial and multiple fulfilments with a delivery journey;
  - idempotent Razorpay webhooks with a CI replay harness;
  - private, revocable order links;
  - two-way order messages.
- **Catalogue:**
  - options and variants (3 × 50, 100 variants);
  - rich-text descriptions, SEO fields and a taxonomy;
  - tags, media (up to 250 per product, alt text, reorder);
  - multi-location inventory with an append-only ledger;
  - bulk actions with outcome reports;
  - safe, audited CSV export.
- **Site Engine:**
  - host resolution and canonical redirects;
  - status pages with correct codes;
  - SEO basics (canonicals, sitemap, robots, product JSON-LD);
  - caching with multi-instance invalidation;
  - the versioned theme contract;
  - atomic draft/publish with frozen versions and revision checks;
  - allow-listed rich text.
- **Dashboard kit:** `PageHeader`, `EmptyState`, `Alert`, form state and
  errors, a command menu, and permission-filtered navigation.
- **Marketing site:** a polished modern SaaS site with a status system,
  pricing from the database with an honest failure state, and the nonce
  CSP.

## 15. Proposed implementation sequence

Principle: **finish functionality → deepen functionality → improve UX →
final visual polish.** Each phase ends with the full gate (format, lint,
typecheck, unit, integration, fresh-build E2E, gitleaks, CI on LTS,
canary and Windows). Tax, invoices, COD and forms each get an ADR before
code. No phase weakens a constraint in §0.
